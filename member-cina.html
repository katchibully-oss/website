My Hobby: Food!

Hi, I'm a food lover. My hobbies include thinking about food, talking about food, cooking food, ordering food, and planning my next meal while eating my current one. I believe calories don't count on weekends, dessert always has space, and sharing food is a very complicated decision. If loving food is wrong, I don't want to be right.

Every moment is an opportunity to plan the next delicious meal. Food is always on the mind — breakfast, lunch, dinner, and everything in between.

Cooking & Experimenting

Ordering & Discovering

The Dessert Philosophy
